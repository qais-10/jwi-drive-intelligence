Questions

Before you reach out

How quickly can we get started?

Most engagements begin with a discovery session within a week of signing. From there, expect a strategy framework within two to three weeks before any creative production starts.

Do you work with our industry?

We have built strategy for retail, hospitality, financial services, wellness, and logistics operators. What matters more than your sector is whether you are ready to commit to a strategy long enough to see it work.

What is included in a quote?

A quote includes a scoped recommendation, a realistic timeline, and a fixed monthly investment range. There is no cost to receive it and no obligation attached.

Do you require long-term contracts?

Our standard engagement runs in ninety day cycles. We ask for one full cycle to give the strategy room to work, and either side can step away at the end of any cycle after that.

How do you measure success?

Every engagement starts by agreeing on the business number we are moving, whether that is qualified leads, revenue per campaign, or repeat purchase rate. We report against that number, not against vanity metrics.

What happens if this does not work for us?

That is what our guarantee is for. If the first ninety days do not produce a documented lift in qualified pipeline, we work the next thirty days at no additional cost until they do.

We have been burned by agencies before, how is this different?

Most agency relationships fail because nobody agreed on a number up front. We fix that on day one, staff your account with our own in-house team instead of subcontractors, and put the guarantee above in writing.

Is this affordable for a business our size?

The Foundations package exists for exactly this reason, a fixed one-time cost with no retainer required. Most clients start there and move into a retainer once they see the roadmap in action.
