Briefing Notes

Before you ask

Q1 What do you actually deliver?

Creative strategy and production: campaigns, content, and media-ready assets built for UAE audiences, produced to the same standard as your global work.

Q2 Which industries do you work with?

FMCG and F&B brands, along with companies across other categories entering the UAE. The process holds regardless of what you sell.

Q3 How long does a launch take?

It depends on scope — a single campaign moves faster than a full market-entry rollout. We’ll give you a real timeline once we know what you’re launching.

Q4 How is this different from a global agency’s regional office, or a local UAE agency?

A global agency’s regional office is often still learning this market. A local agency may not carry your brand’s global standards. We work from both at once — that’s the whole premise.
