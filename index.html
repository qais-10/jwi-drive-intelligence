06 — Before You Book

Common Questions

How is this different from our current agency?

Most agencies rotate junior staff onto your account within a year. At JWI, the senior strategist and creative lead who scope your work are the ones who run it, quarter after quarter.

We're weighing whether to bring creative in-house. Why keep an agency?

An in-house team solves for headcount, not judgement. JWI pairs your business context with senior strategists who've built regional and international campaigns for years, without the overhead of hiring, retaining and managing that team yourself.

Can a GCC-based team really match global creative standards?

JWI's senior leads have built campaigns across the GCC and internationally. Every piece of work is held to one craft standard, adapted locally, not diluted for the region.

Do we need to sign a long-term contract?

No. Project engagements are scoped and fixed. Retainers run month to month, with thirty days' notice to step back, covered under our Continuity Guarantee.
